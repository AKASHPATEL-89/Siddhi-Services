About Us

Siddhi Services — Kanpur's Trusted Entrepreneurship, Marketing & Career Guidance Mentor

We understand that choosing the right project idea, business opportunity, or career path is not simply about selecting a product, service, course, or college — it is a decision that can shape the direction of your entire future.
